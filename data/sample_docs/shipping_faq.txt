Shipping FAQ

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the country. Express
shipping takes 1-2 business days and is available at checkout for an
additional fee.

Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International orders typically take
10-15 business days and may be subject to customs fees charged by the
destination country.

Can I track my order?
Yes. Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email.
You can also view tracking status from your account order history page.

What if my package is lost or damaged?
Contact support with your order number within 14 days of the expected
delivery date. We will investigate with the carrier and issue a
replacement or refund once the claim is confirmed.
