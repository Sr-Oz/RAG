# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase, provided the
product is unused and in its original packaging. Digital products are
eligible for a refund only if they have not been downloaded or activated.

## Process

1. Submit a refund request through the support portal.
2. Include your order number and reason for the return.
3. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original
   payment method.

## Exceptions

Final sale items, gift cards, and custom orders are not eligible for
refunds. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a
defect or shipping error on our part.
